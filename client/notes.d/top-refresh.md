## en
- With Lower refresh rate off, Smoothest video and Balanced with FPS limit ask for the screen's top refresh rate where the frame rate divides it evenly: a 60 FPS stream on a 120 Hz screen asks for 120 Hz instead of dropping the screen to 60 Hz.
- On a screen at 2 to 4 times the frame rate, Smoothest video and Balanced with FPS limit now show every frame for the same number of refreshes: a frame that comes early waits for its turn, which can add delay.
