## en
- Settings opens with a new Recommended section: Stream smoothness (Smoothest, Balanced or Lowest delay), Picture quality (Best or Save data), the screen and refresh rate this device streams at, HDR, and Console mode. Each choice writes the settings below it, so the sections still show every value it changed, and changing one of those by hand reads as Custom.
- Every choice starts on Auto (recommended), which follows this device's screen and decoder, so nothing changes until you pick something; Back to recommended puts the choices back on Auto.
- Console mode opens straight into your games, full screen, for a controller or remote. The Recommended section is in Nightjar for Android and for Windows and Linux alike.
