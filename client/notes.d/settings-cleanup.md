## en
- Settings are tidier: Lower bitrate, Back button, Send system keys and network roaming are one choice each instead of two switches, and Virtual display is a choice of Display arrangement.
- Diagnostics moved to Debug, and a setting that does nothing with your other settings says why.
- Chipset tuning works again: it reaches MediaTek Dimensity and Samsung Exynos decoders once more. It stays off by default.

## desktop
- Keyboard follows text fields is now a setting on the desktop too, on by default.
