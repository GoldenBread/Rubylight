Snapdragon Game Super Resolution 2 (SGSR 2)

The temporal upscaler's accumulate pass (shaders/temporal_accumulate.frag, and its ports in the
PyroWave renderer and Nightjar desktop) adapts the upscale pass of Qualcomm's SGSR 2:
https://github.com/SnapdragonStudios/snapdragon-gsr/blob/d926f074bcb9d714e179f1ce0fcb9ee2eeb5074e/sgsr/v2/include/glsl_2_pass_fs/sgsr2_upscale.fs
Upstream revision: d926f074bcb9d714e179f1ce0fcb9ee2eeb5074e
Shader copyright: Copyright (c) 2024, Qualcomm Innovation Center, Inc. All rights reserved.

Adaptations: decoded video has no depth, engine motion vectors or jitter, so motion comes from
block matching on the decoded luma, the depth-edge term is replaced by one minus the match
confidence, the jitter is zero and history resets on a scene cut; the history holds the bilinear
picture and Catmull-Rom sampling replaces the bilinear history fetch; SGSR 1's edge detail is
added in a separate last pass.
