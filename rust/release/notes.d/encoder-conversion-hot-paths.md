# Encoder, conversion, and audio efficiency
- **Lighter GPU colour conversion and HDR analysis.** The 4:2:0 compute shader now reuses neighbouring chroma samples across each threadgroup instead of re-reading and re-converting adjacent source pixels, 10-bit packed 4:4:4 uses a half-width compute dispatch, 10-bit native 4:4:4 converts Y, U, and V in a single compute pass (keeping the pixel-shader fallback), and HDR luminance statistics and encoder-focus reductions avoid redundant per-pixel work.
- **Event-driven encoder wakeups and leaner AMF, NVENC, PyroWave, and RTX paths.** Encoder output loops wake on completion events rather than polling on short timers where supported, static repeat frames reuse cached conversions and skip redundant uploads, AMF and NVENC coalesce dynamic rate-control updates and reuse frame buffers, NVENC 10-bit native 4:4:4 runs its CUDA plane copy on an asynchronous non-blocking stream, and RTX HDR and RTX Video enhancement hand shared textures across D3D11 and D3D12 with shared fences so downstream conversion can stay on the compute queue.
- **Cached stream encryption and pooled video and PyroWave packetization.** Stream encryption and microphone crypto keep pre-expanded AES-CBC and AES-GCM key schedules instead of expanding them per packet, and video, PyroWave, and bitstream-parsing hot paths reuse pooled buffers to cut per-frame allocations and copies.
- **More efficient host audio processing and capture.** Night Mode dynamics, Binaural headphone surround, PCM conversion and passthrough, and WASAPI audio capture and encoding perform fewer allocations, copies, and redundant calculations per audio packet.
