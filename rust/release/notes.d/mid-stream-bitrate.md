# Bitrate changes during a stream
- **A bitrate set during a stream means the same as the one in the app's settings.** Of the bitrate set in Nightjar the picture gets what is left after error correction, audio and overhead: 14.99 of 20 Mbps. A bitrate Nightjar asked for during the stream, from its stream menu, its automatic bitrate or Keep my bitrate, was taken as the picture's own instead. So choosing the stream's own 20 Mbps raised the picture's bitrate by a third, and a step down of a tenth by Nightjar's automatic bitrate raised it too. The picture now gets what a stream started with that bitrate would have, and the answer to the app names the bitrate it asked for.
