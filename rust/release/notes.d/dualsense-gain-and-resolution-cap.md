# Controller haptics strength and a stream resolution cap
- **DualSense haptics strength.** A game's own waveform haptics on a DualSense with waveform haptics (USB/IP) can be scaled from 0 to 4 times what the game sent, under Sound & controls → Controllers (`dualsense_haptics_gain`, 1 by default); ordinary vibration, the lightbar, adaptive triggers and controller speaker sound keep the game's strength. A device or an app can set its own.
- **Maximum stream resolution.** A host-wide cap keeps a 4K device from streaming 4K to a network or an encoder that cannot carry it: the picture is encoded no larger than the cap while the PC's display still runs at the size the device asked for, with the picture scaled inside the cap (`max_stream_resolution`, native by default). The console's stream card and the host log say when a stream was capped, and its overlay shows the capped resolution.
