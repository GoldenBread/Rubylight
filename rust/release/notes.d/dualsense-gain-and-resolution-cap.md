# Controller haptics strength and a stream resolution cap
- **DualSense haptics strength.** A game's own waveform haptics on a DualSense with waveform haptics (USB/IP) can be scaled from 0 to 4 times what the game sent, under Sound & controls → Controllers (`dualsense_haptics_gain`, 1 by default); ordinary vibration, the lightbar, adaptive triggers and controller speaker sound keep the game's strength. A device or an app can set its own.
- **Maximum stream resolution.** A host-wide cap keeps a 4K device from streaming 4K to a network that cannot carry it: the picture is encoded no larger than the cap while the PC's display still runs at the size the device asked for, and the device scales the smaller picture up, so fine detail softens (`max_stream_resolution`, native by default). The console's stream card and the host log name the coded and display sizes; a Nightjar device that changes size mid-stream can keep the smaller picture as its next request until its resolution is chosen again.
