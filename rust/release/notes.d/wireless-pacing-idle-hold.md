# Wireless burst pacing
- **Wireless burst pacing holds steady through quiet scenes and brief RTT spikes.** For Wi-Fi clients without a reported link speed, Rubylight 6.3.0 stepped automatic burst pacing back down to 2x bitrate during low-bitrate or still scenes and stepped down every 500 ms after a single brief RTT spike instead of waiting between steps, leaving high-refresh streams paced too tightly and causing repeated frame send delays when action resumed (#124). Quiet scenes with healthy control pings and normal RTT now hold the current pacing rate without probing higher or stepping down, and sustained queue-delay or stale-control step-downs re-arm their hold window after each 12.5% step.
