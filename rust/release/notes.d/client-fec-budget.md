# Error correction chosen by the device
- **Nightjar's own error correction no longer costs picture bitrate twice.** With error correction set to automatic or to a fixed share in Nightjar, the PC took its own share off the bitrate set in the app and then the device's share again: at a share of 20 % the picture got a sixth less bitrate than with the PC's own error correction. The device's share now takes the place of the PC's, so the picture gets what it gets with the PC's own error correction unless the device asks for a larger share than the PC's own leaves room for (more than a quarter at the PC's default of 20 %). A bitrate the PC lowered for a weak connection is no longer reduced by the device's share a second time either.
