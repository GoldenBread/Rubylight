# Browser guests over the internet
- **Invite a browser guest from the internet in one step.** A guest who watches or plays in a browser, without Nightjar, needs the guest ports forwarded. In the Stream page's **Guests → Invite**, pick **On the internet**: Rubylight asks your router to forward the guest ports (TCP 47991 and UDP 8002-8005) through UPnP, PCP or NAT-PMP, also with automatic port forwarding off, and puts your public address in the link, so there is nothing to type. Before you send it, the dialog shows each port the router forwarded or refused, your public address, and whether guests on the internet can reach the PC; behind carrier-grade NAT or a router that forwards nothing, it says so and what works instead (Tailscale, IPv6 or forwarding by hand). The ports stay forwarded only while such a link works or a guest watches, and nothing else is forwarded. Guests still click through the browser's certificate warning.
- With automatic port forwarding on, the browser guests' ports are now forwarded only while an invite link works or a guest watches, instead of all the time.
