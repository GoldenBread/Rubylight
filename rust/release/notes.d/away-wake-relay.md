# Away wake relay
- **Wake your PC from away.** A sleeping PC can't answer from the internet, so with **Away wake relay** on (**All settings → Network**, off by default, needs Play over the internet) a paired, always-on device on your home network, such as Nightjar's desktop client on a mini PC, wakes it for Nightjar on your Android phone or tablet when it is away. Nightjar asks that device over the same direct, encrypted path as Play over the internet; it sends the Wake-on-LAN packets on your home network only, only to your PC's own adapters, and only for devices paired with your PC that may start games. No port forwarding, and no server of ours in between.
- **Safe by design.** Every request is signed with a key only your PC hands out, carries a timestamp and a one-time number, and gets at most one short burst of magic packets: a replayed request is answered again but never wakes twice, and a relay sends at most one burst every ten seconds per PC. A relay must have Host control and be on your PC's own network to enroll. Unpairing a device, removing a relay or **Rotate keys** reaches each relay at its next check-in, within five minutes while the PC is awake.
- **Your relays at a glance.** **Settings → Network → Away wake relay** lists each relay with when it last checked in, marks one that stopped refreshing, removes one, rotates the keys, and shows the three steps to add a relay.
