# Invite a friend over the internet
- **Invite a friend to play over the internet.** **Invite a friend** on the console's Home, or in Nightjar's stream menu under **Guests**, makes a link to send to one person. Their Nightjar opens it, pairs as a guest without a PIN and finds your PC over the internet, with no port forwarding and no visit to your network; then they join the game you play once you let them in. The link works once and ends after a day by default; the friend's device gets guest rights only, is always asked about before it joins, and is unpaired when the invite ends. With **Play over the internet** off, the PC looks for invited devices only while an invite needs it.
