# Video after jumbo streams
- **A stock Moonlight client keeps its video while a jumbo-packet client streams.** The shared video socket no longer has its don't-fragment setting turned on by a jumbo stream, so a stock client behind a WireGuard or Tailscale tunnel is never refused for the size of its packets; the jumbo path is still checked by the route's MTU offer and by the PyroWave probe on its own socket. If a jumbo stream's path MTU shrinks while it runs, the two-second route check now shows the network MTU warning on that stream instead of letting its packets fragment unnoticed.
