# RTX on PCs with two GPUs
- **RTX settings say why they are missing.** With the NVIDIA components installed and RTX HDR or RTX Video enhancement still not offered, a setting found by search now gives the reason NVIDIA's check reported, such as a driver too old or a crash in the check, instead of only "Not available on this PC" (issues #103, #111).
- **A stream on another GPU says so.** On a PC with an NVIDIA card beside another GPU, such as a mini PC's Radeon with an NVIDIA card on OCuLink, a stream whose display hangs off the other GPU captures and encodes there, and RTX HDR and RTX Video enhancement can run only on the NVIDIA card. The stream's warning now names the GPU it uses and points to **Settings → Picture → Encoders → Graphics card**, where choosing the NVIDIA card moves capture (with the Automatic capture method) and encoding to it, or says that Desktop Duplication and AMD Direct Capture capture only on the display's GPU, instead of asking to update the NVIDIA driver; the host no longer tries to start them on that GPU (issue #103).
