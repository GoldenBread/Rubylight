# Stream Doctor 2.0
- **Stream Doctor 2.0 for Nightjar.** A paired device may ask which stage of the PC lost its stream's time in the last 30 seconds (capture, conversion, encoding, packetizing or sending), with the late pictures and lost milliseconds behind it, and lower its stream's bitrate by a fifth for this stream only, with an undo that never overrides a change made since. The PC computes nothing new for it: it reads the stutter report the console already keeps, and only while the device asks. Moonlight and other clients see no change.
- **The live stage names the last 30 seconds.** The console's health banner now judges the last 30 seconds, as Nightjar's Stream Doctor does, and says how many new pictures left the PC late and how much of their time each stage lost.
