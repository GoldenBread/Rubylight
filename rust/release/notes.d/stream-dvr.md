# Stream DVR
- **Instant replay and recording.** Turn on **Instant replay** (**Settings → Picture → Stream DVR and clips**, off by default) and the host keeps the last 30 to 120 seconds of the stream in memory: save them as a clip from Clips in Nightjar's Control Center or the console's **Clips** page, or start and stop a recording, without encoding again. Clips are standard MP4 files (H.264, HEVC or AV1 video with Opus sound, keeping the stream's colour and HDR signalling); the console lists, plays, downloads and deletes them, and a quota (20 GB by default) removes the oldest replay clips when full. While it runs the encoder adds a keyframe every 4 seconds; with it off nothing changes. PyroWave streams and passthrough sound cannot be clipped, and the device says so.
