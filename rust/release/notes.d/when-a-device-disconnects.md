# When a device disconnects
- **One choice for what happens when a device disconnects.** The console's Restore displays on disconnect is now When a device disconnects: Restore after the delay (the default) or Keep while paused. If you had restoring turned off, it carries over as Keep while paused, also in each app's and device's own settings. Keep monitors off while paused stays its own setting, and exclusive layouts still bring your monitors back after the delay unless you turn it on.
