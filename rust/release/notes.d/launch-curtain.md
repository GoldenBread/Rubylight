# Launch curtain
- **Launch curtain.** With **Settings → Games → Launch curtain** on (off by default), Nightjar keeps the game's cover over the stream while the game starts, instead of the Windows desktop, store windows and splash screens, and lifts it once the game's window is in front. The PC brings the game to the front four times a second when its store covers it, and a launcher that wants a sign in or an update, or a Windows administrator prompt, shows on the device with **Show PC screen**; the PC never clicks them. The curtain always lifts after 45 seconds and says so. Moonlight and other clients see the stream as before.
- **More launchers recognised.** Launcher prompts and the game's window behind a launcher are now also found for the Xbox app, Battle.net, GOG Galaxy and the Riot Client.
